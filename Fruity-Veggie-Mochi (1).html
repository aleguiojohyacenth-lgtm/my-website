Our Journey & Story

"Fruity Veggie Mochi" was established as an innovative business model targeting health-conscious snack lovers in Carcar City. By combining traditional chewy mochi with vegetable and fruit nutrients, we offer a guilt-free indulgence for everyone!

Our Mission

To provide nutritious, delicious, and affordable fruit-and-vegetable infused mochi snacks to students, families, and professionals in Carcar City, promoting a healthy lifestyle without compromising on fun and flavor.

Our Vision

To become the premier healthy food cart brand in Carcar City and expanded Cebu municipalities, recognized for product innovation, sustainability, and quality customer satisfaction.
